How to Connect
1

Scan or Select

Scan the QR code above or select RGA Guest WiFi in your device Wi-Fi settings.

2

Open Portal

Wait for the welcome screen. If it doesn't appear, open your browser and visit:
neverssl.com or rgagymnast.com

3

Activate Access

Enter your email and tap "Connect for Free" for 60 minutes of access.
Have an Event Pass? Enter your code under "Redeem Pass Coupon".

💡 Quick Tips & Troubleshooting
  • Need More Time? When your 60-min session expires, simply reconnect to RGA Guest WiFi and tap connect again.
  • Portal Not Loading? Toggle Wi-Fi off and back on, or choose "Forget Network" and reconnect.
  • Need Assistance? Please check in with the front desk for questions or event access passes!
